Before you call

How do I know I need a plumber?

Water where it shouldn’t be, a drain that won’t go down, no hot water, or more than one fixture backing up. If you’re not sure, call and describe it.

What should I do while I wait?

If water is running, shut it off at the fixture or the main valve (often in the garage, laundry, or near the water heater). Don’t keep flushing a backed-up toilet.
